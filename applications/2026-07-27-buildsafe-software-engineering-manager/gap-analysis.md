# Gap Analysis — Buildsafe, Software Engineering Manager

## Role summary
Permanent, in-house Software Engineering Manager for Buildsafe (height safety, scaffolding, and
construction technology; 25-year-old national Australian business; office at Arundel, Gold Coast).
Explicitly **not** a hands-on coding role — success is measured by team health, delivery, and
business impact. Leads engineering across BuildCam (proprietary platform), internal operational
systems, and AI initiatives supporting "thousands of active construction sites." First three months
framed as an establishment/assessment phase (understand platforms, build relationships, assess
delivery, set priorities) before driving longer-term modernisation, AI-assisted development adoption,
and architectural decisions — working closely with the Executive team on technology strategy.
