# Senior Project Manager — Bolton Clarke

Bolton Clarke Group one of Australia's largest independent, not-for-profit providers of home care, retirement living and residential aged care, operating nationally and internationally. Bolton Clarke Residential group now has 88 Aged Care Homes across QLD, NSW, VIC, SA & WA, with the inclusion of the Allity, McKenzie & Acacia Living Group.

## About the opportunity

Lead the future of technology in aged care. This high-impact role lets you shape Bolton Clarke's long-term technology strategy, influence executive decision-making, drive enterprise-wide transformation, and deliver innovative solutions that enhance resident wellbeing, operational performance, cyber resilience and future growth across Australia's largest independent aged care provider.

- Lead the development of Bolton Clarke's Residential Aged Care Technology Strategy and future-state technology blueprint.
- Develop multi-year technology roadmaps, business cases and investment recommendations.
- Lead complex enterprise-wide technology transformation programs from planning through delivery.
- Engage and influence executive leaders, operational stakeholders, clinical teams and external partners.
- Drive technology-enabled business transformation to improve resident outcomes and operational efficiency.
- Oversee governance, budgets, risk management, vendor relationships and benefits realisation across strategic programs.
